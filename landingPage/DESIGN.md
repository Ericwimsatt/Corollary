# Corollary Landing Page Design Language

## Chosen direction

Draft Room After Dark is the recommended route. It treats Corollary as a fluorescent analyst layer sitting over a live best-ball room: ink-black surfaces, fluorescent lime and hot magenta signals, compact mono labels, hard dividers, synthetic draft-board rows, and oversized compressed headlines.

## Surface strategy

This is a Persuade surface for degen best-ball drafters. The first viewport leads with a memorable promise, “Make the draft feel slower,” then proves the mechanism with an illustrative live draft panel. The page offers eight visual directions as a decision surface; the recommended fifth direction is the strongest fit for the brief.

## Tokens and materials

- Background: near-black ink `#070809` / `#090b0d`
- Primary signal: fluorescent lime `#d9ff35`
- Secondary signal: hot magenta `#ff3f9e`
- Supporting signals: cyan `#4cf0e8`, violet `#8a6cff`, orange `#ff7b31`
- Borders: dark analyst-grid lines around `#2a2e2d`
- Typography: compressed, high-impact system sans for display; compact monospace for measured labels and draft data
- Shape: 12–18px working surfaces, 7–10px controls, circular palette swatches
- Motion: restrained lift/brightness on primary actions; keep the draft artifact visible by default

## Content rules

Show roster construction first, ranking signals second, and playoff stacks third. Label synthetic draft data as illustrative. Do not invent benchmarks, customers, testimonials, pricing, or platform partnerships.
